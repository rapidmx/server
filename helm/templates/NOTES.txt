Real inbound/outbound mail (Postfix, DKIM signing, postfix-bridge) is a separate chart - install
https://github.com/rapidmx/postfix-bridge alongside this release, with its `mail.ingestBaseUrl` pointed at
this release's own "{{ include "rrst.fullname" . }}-services" Service and its `mail.ingestSecret` set to
the exact same value as this release's own `mail.ingestSecret` (stored in the
"{{ include "rrst.fullname" . }}-mail-ingest-secret" Secret), or postfix-bridge's calls to /internal/mta will fail
authorization. /internal/* is not routed through the Gateway; postfix-bridge must call the Service directly. Read it with:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get secret {{ include "rrst.fullname" . }}-mail-ingest-secret -o jsonpath='{.data.mail__transport__ingest__secret}' | base64 -d
